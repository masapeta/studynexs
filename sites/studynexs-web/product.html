StudyNexs transforms educational institutions into intelligent, connected ecosystems through AI-powered administration, teaching, learning, communication, and analytics.

The exam loop

Our recommended pilot path — prove value in one term before expanding.

1. Curriculum-grounded papers

Syllabus, blueprint, and chapter weightage feed AI question paper generation. Teachers approve before print.

2. Conduct & capture

Offline exams as today. Teachers upload answer-sheet images when ready.

3. AI suggests, teacher decides

Vision OCR transcribes answers; AI suggests marks from your rubric. Zero marks publish without explicit approve.

4. Learn from mistakes

Weak-topic reports and opt-in tutor lessons target flagged misconceptions — not open-ended chat.

Built for every role

Principals

Dashboard visibility, exam readiness, and management reports — not a full ERP replacement on day one.

Teachers

AI papers, marks, report remarks, and mastery heatmaps — always editable before publish.

Parents & students

Progress views, fee status, and mistake-recovery tutor — opt-in during pilot.

Human-in-the-loop by design

StudyNexs never auto-publishes consequential AI outputs. Question papers, marks, report card remarks, and parent-facing narratives require explicit teacher or admin approval. AI proposes; your staff decides.
